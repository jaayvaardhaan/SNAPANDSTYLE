The engine

Personal, visual, deliberately simple.

Snap&Style guides one automatic face capture, lets you choose Retake or Proceed, sends an approved JPEG to the CV API, and receives facial features as JSON. No camera photographs are saved by this workflow. Your selected preferences and CV features then rank real clothing items from the catalog.

01
Live cameraMediaPipe-based face alignment and automatic capture.
02
Direct processingJPEG is processed in memory; no folder picker, image archive, or queue is needed.
03
Outfit directionAppearance estimates inform suggested colors, while the user controls occasion, season, category and style.
